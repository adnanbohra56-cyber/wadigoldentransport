About us

A transportation partner built around reliability.

Wadi Golden Transport is a Chandrapur-based transportation firm serving industrial and commercial customers since 1976. With offices at Chandrapur, Nagpur Wadi, Nagpur Lakadganj and Ballarpur, we coordinate dependable road freight and fleet movement for businesses across India.

Our operating focus is simple: move material safely, keep customers informed and coordinate each shipment from booking to delivery.

Built for B2B logistics.

From industrial loads and steel-related cargo to FMCG and auto parts, our network is designed around business-to-business transportation requirements.
